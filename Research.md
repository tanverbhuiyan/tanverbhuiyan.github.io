## 🔍 Research Interests 
---

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning  
I am passionate about developing **data-driven and AI-powered solutions** that are interpretable, reliable, and impactful. My work focuses on:  
- **Predictive Modeling & Statistical Learning** – building robust ML models for classification, forecasting, and decision-making.  
- **Explainable AI (XAI)** – improving model interpretability to ensure transparency and trust in real-world applications.  
- **Human-Centered AI** – designing systems that keep humans in the loop, ensuring fairness, accountability, and ethical deployment.  

### 💰 FinTech & Financial Analytics  
I explore how **AI and data science can transform financial systems** by improving decision-making, reducing risk, and driving innovation. My interests include:  
- **Credit Risk Modeling** and **Fraud Detection** using machine learning and anomaly detection techniques.  
- **Algorithmic Trading & Portfolio Optimization** powered by statistical modeling and reinforcement learning.  
- **Financial Inclusion** – leveraging AI to create accessible, data-driven financial services for underserved populations.  

### 🏥 Public Health & Social Good  
I aim to apply statistical and machine learning methods to **improve healthcare outcomes and public policy**. My research explores:  
- **Epidemiological Modeling** – understanding patterns of disease spread and intervention effectiveness.  
- **Health Data Analytics** – extracting insights from clinical, demographic, and behavioral data for better decision-making.  
- **Fair & Safe AI in Healthcare** – mitigating model biases and ensuring equitable, patient-centered applications.
