## 🔍 Research Interests 
---

  
I aim to apply statistical and machine learning methods to **improve healthcare outcomes and public policy**. My research explores:  
- **Epidemiological Modeling** – understanding patterns of disease spread and intervention effectiveness.  
- **Health Data Analytics** – extracting insights from clinical, demographic, and behavioral data for better decision-making.  
- **Fair & Safe AI in Healthcare** – mitigating model biases and ensuring equitable, patient-centered applications.
- **Predictive Modeling & Statistical Learning** – building robust ML models for classification, forecasting, and decision-making.
